Components

The built library in manifest order, rendered from its real CSS with every defined modifier and interaction state.

Card

Groups closely related content and actions within a bounded surface.

When not to use: Do not use it only to decorate content that already forms a coherent page section.

Default

Standard spacing and elevation.

Related content belongs here.

Footer

Compact

Reduced gap and padding.

card--compact

Flat

The border remains while elevation is removed.

card--flat

Compact and flat

Both modifiers compose.

card--compact card--flat

<article class="card" aria-labelledby="card-example-title">
  <header class="card__header">
    <h2 class="card__title" id="card-example-title">Card title</h2>
    <p class="card__description">A concise description of the grouped content.</p>
  </header>
  <div class="card__body">
    <p class="card__text">Card content belongs here.</p>
  </div>
  <footer class="card__footer">
    <p class="card__text">Optional supporting information or actions belong here.</p>
  </footer>
</article>

Section

Organizes a distinct topic or task within the page flow.

When not to use: Do not use it for a small group that belongs inside another component.

A distinct topic

The header introduces content that belongs together in page flow.

Section content belongs here.

<section class="section" aria-labelledby="section-example-title">
  <header class="section__header">
    <h2 class="section__title" id="section-example-title">Section title</h2>
    <p class="section__description">A concise introduction to this topic.</p>
  </header>
  <div class="section__body">
    <p>Section content belongs here.</p>
  </div>
</section>

Page header

Introduces the current page with its title, context, and primary actions.

When not to use: Do not use it as a promotional hero or repeat it within page sections.

Workspace settings

Manage defaults for this workspace.

<header class="page-header">
  <div class="page-header__content">
    <h1 class="page-header__title">Page title</h1>
    <p class="page-header__description">A concise description of the page's purpose.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="page-header__actions">
    <button class="button" type="button">Primary action</button>
  </div>
</header>

Empty state

Explains why no content is present and offers an appropriate next action.

When not to use: Do not use it while content is still loading or when an error needs explicit recovery.

No results

Try changing your search or filters.

i

Nothing saved

Saved items will appear here.

<section class="empty-state" aria-labelledby="empty-state-title">
  <h2 class="empty-state__title" id="empty-state-title">No results</h2>
  <p class="empty-state__description">Try changing your search or filters.</p>
</section>

Loading

Communicates that an active operation has not yet completed.

When not to use: Do not use it when no work is in progress or as a substitute for an empty state.

Loading records
<span class="loading" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></span>

Dialog

Focuses attention on content or actions that require a response.

When not to use: Do not use it for nonblocking feedback or content that belongs in the page flow.

Default

Confirm change?

Review the details before continuing.

Danger icon
!

Remove item?

This action cannot be undone.

<dialog class="dialog" aria-labelledby="dialog-title" aria-describedby="dialog-description">
  <form method="dialog">
    <div class="dialog__body">
      <div>
        <h2 class="dialog__title" id="dialog-title">Remove item?</h2>
        <p class="dialog__description" id="dialog-description">This action cannot be undone.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="dialog__actions">
      <button class="button button--quiet" type="submit" value="cancel">Cancel</button>
      <button class="button button--danger" type="submit" value="confirm">Remove</button>
    </div>
  </form>
</dialog>

Inline message

Communicates contextual status or feedback within the current flow.

When not to use: Do not use it for blocking confirmations or unrelated global notifications.

Danger

<div class="inline-message inline-message--danger" role="alert">
  <span>The item could not be saved.</span>
</div>

Input

Captures one line of user-entered text or data.

When not to use: Do not use it for multiline content or a fixed set of choices.

<label for="display-name">Display name</label>
<input class="input" id="display-name" type="text" placeholder="Enter a name">

Toolbar

Groups compact controls that operate on the current view or content.

When not to use: Do not use it for page navigation or unrelated actions.

  <button class="button button--quiet" type="button">Redo</button>
