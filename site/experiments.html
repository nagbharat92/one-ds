Spinner motion lab

An experiment for tuning the spinner. Two points ride one eased orbit — slow over the top, fast through the bottom — with the tail delayed behind the head. Drag the controls, then port the numbers into the spinner component.

Preview and controls

The preview is drawn live in SVG so the motion updates as you drag. The shipped spinner bakes these same numbers into CSS keyframes.

Cycle time 1000 ms
Gap / arc size 20%
Delay between the two points. Small is a short arc, large is a long comet tail.
Exaggeration 0.65
How much it slows at the top and speeds through the bottom. Zero is a constant rate.
Size 128 px
Thickness 4
Rail opacity 0.20
arc 28°–116° 4.7× top to bottom
These are the values shipped in the spinner component today: cycle 1000 ms, gap 20%, exaggeration 0.65, arc 28°–116°.
